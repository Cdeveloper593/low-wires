# Low Wires

A scroll-driven walk through a quiet lo-fi neighbourhood, from a laundry-strung rooftop at 04:48 to the same roof at dawn.

As you scroll, one continuous camera glide carries you down the stairs, past the bicycles and the vending machine's hum, to the convenience store for tea, across the river bridge under a shooting star, and home again — no cuts, every scene handing off to the next from its actual last frame.

Built with the scroll-world scrub engine: six Veo 3.1 Lite clips (Architecture A — a single forward walkthrough), generated in Google Flow from hand-drawn-style anime stills, encoded for smooth scroll-scrubbing (H.264, CRF 20, GOP 8).

Planned by GPT-6.2 Astra (Max), coded by GPT-6.2 Sol.

Open `index.html` — no build step.
